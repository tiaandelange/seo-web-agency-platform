# Support packages (recurring services)

Sold monthly, cancel with 30 days’ notice (proposed term — confirm). Hosting and site-care plans share one public page (`/services/website-maintenance-and-support/`) to avoid thin sibling pages. Search Care is a **separate** service URL (`/services/search-care/`).

| Plan | For | Core inclusions | Published /month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential care | Starter sites | Hosting admin, updates, backups, monitoring, small edits | R850–R1,500 (indicative) |
| Business support | Lead-gen sites | Essential + change hours, form monitoring, Search Console review | R1,800–R3,500 (indicative) |
| System & store support | Ecommerce / apps | SLA targets, payment/workflow monitoring, dependency updates | R3,000–R8,000 (indicative) |
| Measurement & Reporting | Any client with healthy tracking | Monthly conversion reporting, Looker Studio upkeep, plain-English readout | **R1,250** (fixed add-on) |
| Search Care | Owners wanting capped SEO health | GSC/GA review, capped technical/on-page fixes, reporting | **R3,950** (fixed; `/services/search-care/`) |

## Deferred

Content & SEO support (1–2 mapped articles/pages + metadata) at R4,500–R9,000 remains **not sold at launch** until capacity exists. Search Care is the honest entry monthly product.

## Rules

1. Support attaches at proposal stage of every build.
2. Unused hours don’t roll over.
3. No plan promises rankings; reporting shows real GSC/GA data only.
4. Out-of-plan work at published hourly rate (R650–R950/h).
